3. Minor children

If there are minor children, the settlement must keep their care, contact, maintenance, and best interests visible.

Children questions

  • Names and ages
  • Primary residence
  • Care and contact
  • Maintenance amount
  • School fees
  • Medical aid

Family Advocate checklist

  • Parenting plan attached
  • Recommendation requested
  • Recommendation received
  • Endorsement complete
Unopposed route rule When settlement is complete and the Family Advocate path is complete where needed, the page can mark the matter as suitable for an unopposed or simplified hearing route.

4. Settlement summary

This summary is the plain-language view of the agreement and will later support drafting and user explanations.

Example summary

  • Property sold and proceeds divided equally
  • Vehicle retained by Defendant
  • Pension interests split as agreed
  • Maintenance included for minor children
  • Parenting plan attached
  • Family Advocate endorsement pending
Readiness note If the endorsement or a key clause is missing, the page should remain amber and show exactly what still needs to be completed.

5. Settlement questions

The user should be able to ask settlement-related questions from this page.

Can we settle after summons?

Yes. Settlement can happen at almost any stage, and the system should always keep the settlement tab open.

Do I need Family Advocate approval?

If minor children are involved, the Family Advocate path should be tracked and completed where required.

What makes the matter ready for unopposed set-down?

The settlement, children, pension, and endorsement items must be complete enough to move the dashboard to a simplified path.

This settlement centre supports procedural assistance and access-to-justice guidance. It does not provide legal advice.
