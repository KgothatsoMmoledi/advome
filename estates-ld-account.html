1. Draft the Liquidation and Distribution account

Use the estate inventory, claims, liabilities, and beneficiaries to build the draft account.

Drafting

What the L&D should contain

  • Assets and values
  • Debts and liabilities
  • Creditor claims
  • Beneficiary entitlements
  • Proposed distribution

Draft status checks

  • Assets confirmed
  • Claims recorded
  • Objections reviewed
  • Distribution calculations prepared

2. Lodge and inspect

The account should be lodged with the Master and then opened for inspection and objections.

3. Respond to issues

Keep the file open until any objection or Master query is resolved.

Common issues

  • Incorrect beneficiary share
  • Missing valuation
  • Unresolved claim
  • Document mismatch

What the user should do

  • Correct the account
  • Resubmit if required
  • Track the inspection period
  • Prepare for approval

4. Distribution readiness

Once the inspection period ends with no unresolved issues, the estate can move to distribution and closure.

Pending approval
Distribution readiness The L&D account is not yet final. The user still needs to complete the inspection stage and resolve any remaining objections or queries.

5. Questions panel

The user should be able to ask practical L&D account questions directly from this page.

What goes into the L&D account?

It should set out estate assets, liabilities, claims, and how the estate will be distributed.

What happens during inspection?

The account is available for inspection so interested parties can object if they need to.

What if the Master asks for changes?

Keep the account open, correct the issue, and resubmit as needed until approval is possible.

What happens after approval?

The workflow should move into final distribution and closure of the estate.
