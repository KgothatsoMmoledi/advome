📚 Relevant Legislation
Administration of Estates Act 66 of 1965 Sections 1-35
Governs the administration of deceased estates, appointment of executors, inventory requirements, and distribution procedures. The Master's Office oversees all estate administrations in South Africa.
Primary Legislation Master's Office
Wills Act 7 of 1953 Sections 1-6
Governs the validity, execution, and interpretation of wills. A will must comply with formal requirements to be valid.
Primary Legislation
Intestate Succession Act 81 of 1987 Sections 1-5
Governs distribution where the deceased died without a valid will. Spouse and children are primary beneficiaries.

Estates Questions

What documents does the Master's Office need?
Death notice, death certificate, ID documents, marriage certificate, will (if any), and covering letter. Requirements vary by office. Verify before lodging.
How long does estate administration take?
Simple estates: 6-12 months. Complex estates with disputes or property sales: 2-3 years or more.
What is the L&D account?
Liquidation and Distribution account. It lists all assets, liabilities, and shows how the net estate will be distributed to heirs.
