Record Questions

Was the arbitration recorded?
CCMA arbitrations are digitally recorded. Request the audio from the CCMA regional office.
Do I need the full record or necessary portions?
Rule 37(12): necessary portions for ordinary review issues. Complete record if arguing unreasonableness or irrationality.
Has the transcript been edited?
The draft transcript must be checked for accuracy, especially witness names, dates, and technical terms.
