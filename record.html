Request the certified arbitration record within 60 days
⏰ CRITICAL DEADLINE

You have 60 days from the date the Registrar notifies you that the record is received to file the transcribed record (Rule 37(9)-(14)). The 60-day period only starts once a complete record has been filed by the CCMA.

Email or visit the CCMA office where your arbitration was held. Request the certified record of arbitration proceedings.

Include your CCMA case number, arbitrator name, and date of arbitration.

The CCMA may charge a fee for transcribing and certifying the record. Ask for a cost estimate upfront.

Collect the record in person or arrange delivery. Ensure it is certified by the CCMA. The Registrar will now notify all parties via Court Online and SharePoint.

Transcribe & Paginate
Rule 23(3) requirements for the complete file
📋 Pagination Requirements (Rule 23(3))

• Number every page consecutively from page 1
• Use Arabic numerals at bottom centre
• Include an index at the front
• Bind documents in chronological order
• Volumes of not more than 120 pages

Tip: If the record is very long, split into multiple volumes. Label each clearly (e.g. "Volume 1 of 3").
