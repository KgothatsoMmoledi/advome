Frequently asked questions

Common queries about the Labour Court set-down process.

When must I apply for a hearing date?

Within 10 court days after indexing and paginating the file (Rule 37(25)). The deadline is calculated using court days, excluding weekends, public holidays, and the December/January recess.

What if the matter is unopposed?

The same rule applies. For unopposed reviews, you may apply for a hearing date within 10 court days after the last day for filing an answering affidavit (Rule 37(26)).

How do I file the request?

You can file via the Court Online portal (upload the request under your case) or email the Registrar directly. Always keep a copy of the submission.

Do I need to serve the request on the other parties?

Normally, the court will notify all parties once a date is allocated. However, it is good practice to serve a copy of the request on the respondents and file proof of that service.

What if the Registrar doesn’t respond?

Follow up after 10 court days. If still no response, escalate to the Senior Registrar or the court manager. Keep a record of all communication.

What happens after a hearing date is allocated?

You must file Heads of Argument within 15 court days (opposed matters) or before the hearing (unopposed). Then prepare for the oral hearing.
