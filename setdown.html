Apply for Hearing Date — Manual Steps
Rule 37(25) — 10 court days after indexing and paginating
⏰ 10-DAY DEADLINE

You must apply within 10 court days after indexing and paginating the file. Use the deadline tracker below.

📖 The Law

Rule 37(25): After indexing and paginating, the applicant must apply to the Registrar for a hearing date within 10 court days.

Rule 37(26): For unopposed matters, the applicant may apply within 10 court days after the last day for filing an answering affidavit.

  • Log in to the Court Online Portal at https://courtonline.co.za.
  • Navigate to My Cases → Select your case.
  • Click "Apply for Hearing Date" or "Set Down".
  • Select hearing type: Review (or Opposed/Unopposed Motion).
  • Upload the complete paginated file (Sections A, B, C).
  • Confirm service and file the request.
📝 What Happens Next?
  1. The Registrar allocates a hearing date and notifies all parties.
  2. You must file Heads of Argument within 15 court days (opposed) or before the hearing (unopposed).
  3. Prepare your oral argument and attend court.
