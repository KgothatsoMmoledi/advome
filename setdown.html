⏰ 10-DAY DEADLINE — Rule 37(25)

You must apply to the Registrar for a hearing date within 10 court days after indexing and paginating the file. If you miss this deadline, your matter may be delayed or struck from the roll.

📖 The Law

Rule 37(25): After indexing and paginating, the applicant must apply to the Registrar for a hearing date within 10 court days.

Rule 37(26): For unopposed matters, the applicant may apply for a hearing date within 10 court days after the last day for filing an answering affidavit.

Rule 40: Heads of Argument must be filed within 15 court days after pleadings close (for opposed reviews).

  • Log in to the Court Online Portal at https://courtonline.co.za.
  • Navigate to My Cases → Select your case.
  • Look for "Apply for Hearing Date" or "Set Down" option.
  • Select the type of hearing: Unopposed Motion / Opposed Motion / Review.
  • Upload the complete paginated file (all sections, properly indexed).
  • Confirm that all parties have been served and the proof of service is filed.
  • Enter your digital signature and submit.
  • The Registrar will allocate a hearing date and notify all parties.

📝 What Happens Next?

After applying for a hearing date:

  1. The Registrar allocates a hearing date and notifies all parties;
  2. You must file Heads of Argument within 15 court days (opposed) or before the hearing (unopposed);
  3. Prepare your oral argument for the hearing;
  4. Attend the hearing on the allocated date.
