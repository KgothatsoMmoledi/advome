Build rule

What this page must do

Finalisation should not hide the result. It should present the outcome and any remaining obligations clearly.

  • Show the resultMake the judgment or settlement outcome obvious.
  • Track any follow-upCompliance, costs, or archiving should remain visible.
  • Close the chainMark the labour review flow as complete when nothing is outstanding.
