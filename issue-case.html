This button is on your dashboard. Select "Labour Court" as the court type.

Choose the appropriate category:

  • Review of CCMA Award — Section 145 LRA
  • Appeal on Point of Law — Section 145A LRA
  • Reinstatement Application
  • Other Labour Court Application

Enter all parties exactly as on your Notice of Motion:

  • Applicant (your name)
  • Respondent (employer name)
  • Second Respondent (arbitrator name)
  • Third Respondent (CCMA)

Review all details. Click "Submit" to create the case. You will receive a case reference number immediately.

⏰ CRITICAL: Case Reference Number

Write down your case reference number immediately. Format: JS1234/2024. You will need it for all future filings.
