Questions

The user should be able to ask practical pre-trial questions from this page.

What happens during pre-trial?

The page should explain the narrowing of issues, settlement checks, witness preparation, and trial bundle readiness.

Can we settle now?

Yes. Settlement should always remain available and visible, even at pre-trial stage.

Do I need witnesses?

The witness centre should show whether witnesses are required, ready, or still missing.

What documents go into the trial bundle?

The evidence centre and bundle checklist should show the documents that must be included before set-down.
