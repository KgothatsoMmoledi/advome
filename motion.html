Motion Questions

What relief do you want?
Set aside (quash the award), remit (send back to CCMA), or substitute (replace with your preferred order).
Was service completed?
Proof of service must be uploaded. The respondent must be properly served to start the opposition clock.
Did the other side oppose?
Track notice of intention to oppose and answering affidavit separately. Only the latter means full opposition.
