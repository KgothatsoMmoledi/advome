Step 6 of 21 — Labour Court Review Application

Labour Court
Notice of Motion
Formal application to review the CCMA award
⏰ CRITICAL: 6-Week Deadline

You have 6 weeks from the date the award was served on you to file the review application in the Labour Court (LRA s145, Rule 37).

This is the formal application to the Labour Court to review or appeal a CCMA award. You must cite the arbitrator and the CCMA as parties.

IN THE LABOUR COURT OF SOUTH AFRICA
HELD AT [JOHANNESBURG]
NOTICE OF MOTION
Case Number: [To be assigned by Court Online]

[Applicant Name]

[Respondent Name]

Third Respondent
Founding Affidavit
Main document supporting your application

The Founding Affidavit must set out the facts, legal grounds for review, and attach the complete award. Rule 37(2) requires a concise statement of grounds — no more than necessary.

📎 Required Attachments

1. Complete certified copy of the arbitration award
2. CCMA case record (if available)
3. Any relevant correspondence

Document Checklist
Before proceeding to Court Online
  • Notice of Motion (drafted and reviewed)
  • Founding Affidavit (complete with all grounds)
  • Certified copy of arbitration award attached
  • Annexures properly marked and paginated
  • Proof of service on CCMA (if required)
  • All party details confirmed and correct
Tip: Save drafts before uploading to Court Online. The portal does not auto-save.
