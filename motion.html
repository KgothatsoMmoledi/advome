Court Online Registration & Filing

Step-by-step guide to register and file your review application on the Court Online portal. Directive 1 of 2025 makes this compulsory for all Labour Court matters.

8 Steps to File on Court Online

1
Register at courtonline.co.za

Select "Individual". SA ID required. Foreign nationals: contact the Court Online Help Desk for manual registration.

2
Verify your email

Check your inbox for an activation link. Click it to verify your account.

3
Sign in and click "Start a Case"

Log into your account. On the dashboard, click the "Start a Case" button.

4
Select "Labour Court" + correct seat

Choose the correct Labour Court seat: Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, or Gqeberha. This must match where the arbitration took place or where the respondent is based.

5
⚠ Upload ONLY the Notice of Motion (PDF)

Do NOT upload the Founding Affidavit at this stage. Uploading the affidavit now will cause the Registrar to REJECT your application. The Registrar needs the Notice of Motion first to issue a case number.

6
Wait for Registrar email with case number

The Registrar will review your Notice of Motion and issue an official case number via email (usually within 1–3 business days). This is your real case number — not a reference number.

7
Upload remaining documents under "My Case Documents"

Once the case number is issued, go to "My Cases" → select your case → upload your Founding Affidavit, copy of the arbitration award, and proof of service under "My Case Documents".

8
Pay filing fees via Court Online portal

Pay by EFT or card. Keep the payment confirmation. If you cannot afford fees, apply for indigent status under Rule 70 (contact the Registrar with proof of income).

Responding Affidavit (10 Working Days After Service)

The respondent has 10 working days after service to file a Notice of Intention to Oppose and an Answering Affidavit. Determine if your review is defended or undefended.

Did you receive a responding affidavit?

Check your email and Court Online "My Case Documents" for any documents uploaded by the respondent(s) within 10 working days of service.

Your Review is Undefended

The respondent has not filed a Notice of Intention to Oppose or Answering Affidavit within 10 working days of service. The matter may proceed on the unopposed roll.

You still need to file Heads of Argument (Rule 40) before the matter can be set down for hearing. The court will consider your review based solely on your founding papers.

What a Responding Affidavit looks like (so you know what you DIDN'T receive): 
IN THE LABOUR COURT OF SOUTH AFRICA

Case No: [CASE NUMBER]

In the matter between:

[EMPLOYEE NAME]          Applicant

and

[EMPLOYER NAME]          Respondent

ANSWERING AFFIDAVIT

I, the undersigned [RESPONDENT REP], state under oath:

1. I am the [position] of the Respondent. I have full
   knowledge of the facts herein.

2. I deny the allegations in paragraphs [X] to [Y] of the
   Applicant's founding affidavit.

3. The commissioner correctly applied the law and the evidence
   supports the award. [Brief reasons].

4. The application for review should be dismissed with costs.

Dated at ____________ on [DATE].

_____________________
Deponent

SWORN TO before me...

Your Review is Defended

The respondent has filed an Answering Affidavit. You now have 5 working days from receipt to file a Replying Affidavit (Rule 7(5)(a)). You may only address new issues raised in the answering affidavit — no new facts are allowed.

Example of an Answering Affidavit Structure: 
IN THE LABOUR COURT OF SOUTH AFRICA

Case No: [CASE NUMBER]

In the matter between:

[EMPLOYEE NAME]          Applicant

and

[EMPLOYER NAME]          Respondent

ANSWERING AFFIDAVIT

I, the undersigned [NAME], do hereby make oath and state:

1. I am the [position] of the Respondent. I have full
   knowledge of the facts herein and am duly authorised to
   depose to this affidavit.

2. I deny the allegations in paragraphs [X] to [Y] of the
   Applicant's founding affidavit. The commissioner correctly
   applied the test in Sidumo v Rustenburg Platinum and the
   evidence supports the award.

3. The Applicant's claim that [specific allegation] is
   incorrect because [factual rebuttal].

4. The application for review should be dismissed with costs.

Dated at ____________ on [DATE].

_____________________
[NAME]
Deponent

SWORN TO before me at ____________ on [DATE], the deponent
having acknowledged that he/she knows and understands the
contents of this affidavit and that the facts stated herein
are true and correct.

_____________________
Commissioner of Oaths

Generate Your Replying Affidavit (Rule 7(5)(a))

Address ONLY new issues raised in the answering affidavit. No new facts. No repetition of your founding affidavit.

5-Day Deadline Warning

You must file your replying affidavit within 5 working days of receiving the answering affidavit. Failure to do so may result in the court considering the matter on the respondent's version alone. File on Court Online under "My Case Documents" and serve all parties by email.
