⏰ RESPONDENT DEADLINE

The respondent has 10 days from the date of service to file a Notice of Intention to Oppose (Rule 35(6)). If they miss this deadline, the matter proceeds as undefended.

  • Notice of Intention to Oppose
  • Address for Service
As the Applicant: You do not need to do anything at this stage. Wait for the 10-day period to expire. If you receive a Notice of Opposition, proceed to the "Record" step. If not, proceed to the "Undefended" path.
✅ Opposition Received

The respondent has filed a Notice of Intention to Oppose. You must now request the CCMA record and proceed to the next step.

⚠️ Undefended Application

No opposition was filed within 10 days. You may apply for default judgment. See the "Undefended Path" tab.

Undefended Application Path
No Notice of Opposition filed within 10 days
Default Judgment Process

1. File a certificate of non-opposition (affidavit)
2. Apply for default judgment on Court Online
3. Registrar may grant judgment without a hearing
4. Or matter may be set down for unopposed hearing

CERTIFICATE OF NON-OPPOSITION

I, [Applicant Name], do hereby certify that:

1. The application in the above matter was served on the respondent on [date].

2. More than 10 days have elapsed since the date of service.

3. No Notice of Intention to Oppose has been received from the respondent.

4. I therefore apply for the matter to be dealt with as undefended.

Note: Even in undefended matters, you may still need to file the transcribed record if the court requires it. Check with the Registrar.
