What the portal will do

  • Identify the current stage of the case
  • Show the next step in plain language
  • Track missing documents and deadlines
  • Show settlement and children flags where relevant
  • Support a case-health score for each matter
  • Prepare users for hearing or trial

How to use Advome

  • Choose the matter type first
  • Enter or upload the main documents later
  • Follow the next-step panel on every page
  • Use the dashboard to see urgent matters first

Roadmap

The first working modules are Divorce, CCMA, Dashboard, and Attorney Portal. Other matters can be added later without changing the main structure.

Advome supports access to justice, procedural clarity, and practical case navigation across matters.
