Ask about this step

The health page should help the user understand the next move, not just show a percentage.

How do I prepare discovery?

The page should guide the user through the discovery affidavit, what must be disclosed, and what must be attached.

What if the other side ignores discovery?

The dashboard should mark the matter as delayed and show the option to compel or follow up procedurally.

Can I compel discovery?

The page should explain the procedural route and show the user what documents they need before making that move.

What documents should I disclose?

The document centre and health centre should work together to show the documents tied to discovery.
