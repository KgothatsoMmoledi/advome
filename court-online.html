Labour Court
Court Online Registration
Compulsory electronic filing system for the Labour Court
Court Online is mandatory from 14 April 2025. All new Labour Court cases must be initiated through the portal.

Go to courtonline.co.za and click "Register"

Choose "Individual" if representing yourself. Choose "Organisation" if you are a legal representative or company.

Enter your personal details exactly as they appear on your ID:

  • Full name and surname
  • ID number
  • Cell phone number (for OTP)
  • Email address
  • Physical address

You will receive an OTP via SMS. Enter it to verify your cell phone. Then check your email for an activation link.

Read and accept the Court Online terms. This is a legal requirement before you can file any case.

Important: Use the same email address throughout the case. All court notifications will be sent to this address.
Profile Setup
Complete your profile to enable case filing
Digital Signature: On Court Online, type your name in UPPERCASE in the signature block. This is legally valid under the ECT Act.
Common Issues
Solutions to frequent Court Online problems

Check that your cell number is correct. Wait 2 minutes and click "Resend OTP". If still nothing, contact Court Online support: 012 492 8200

Documents must be PDF format, under 10MB per file. Ensure your PDF is not password-protected or scanned at too high resolution.

You may have registered before. Try "Forgot Password" or contact support with your ID number to recover the account.

After 3 failed login attempts, your account is locked for 30 minutes. Wait and try again, or reset your password.
