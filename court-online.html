Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I file manually at the court instead of using Court Online?

A: No. As of 14 April 2025, Directive 1 of 2025 makes Court Online mandatory for all new Labour Court cases. Manual issuing is prohibited.

Q: What if I don't have a computer or internet access?

A: Visit the Court Online Service Desk at the Labour Court. Staff will assist you with registration and filing free of charge. Directive 1.7

Q: What is the difference between the reference number and case number?

A: The reference number (e.g., HCGS00363) is generated by Court Online when you click "Start a Case." The case number (e.g., J1234/2026) is issued by the Registrar after approving your Notice of Motion. Only the case number goes on court documents. Directive 1.8–1.10

Q: Can I upload the Founding Affidavit with the Notice of Motion when starting the case?

A: No. Upload ONLY the Notice of Motion for issuing. The Founding Affidavit is uploaded later under "My Case Documents" after the case number is issued. Uploading the affidavit at the start will cause rejection. Directive 1.11

Q: How long does the Registrar take to issue a case number?

A: Usually 1–3 business days, depending on volume and compliance. You will receive an email notification.

Q: Is there a fee for using Court Online?

A: No. Registration, filing, and document uploads are free for all users. The Labour Court does not charge filing fees for review applications by self-represented litigants.

Q: What if the document type I need is not in the dropdown?

A: Do NOT select "Other." Email CourtOnlineSupport@judiciary.org.za with the subject "Request to add a new document type on Court Online" and describe the document. Directive 1.12(d)–(e)

Q: Can I serve documents by email?

A: Yes. Under Labour Court Rule 9(1)(a)(iv), service by email is sufficient if the other party has an email address. You must file an affidavit of service proving delivery. Rule 9(1)(a)(iv)
