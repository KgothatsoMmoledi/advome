Review decision panel

Questions the engine should ask

  • Are you happy with the award?If yes, the matter can move to finalisation. If no, open the review track.
  • What happened?Capture the user’s explanation in neutral language.
  • What went wrong?Identify procedural issues, ignored evidence, or jurisdiction concerns.
  • Do you have audio or notes?Allow the user to upload or flag recordings, notes, and transcripts.
