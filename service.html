Step 9 of 21 — Labour Court Review Application

Labour Court
Service of Application
Serve on all respondents within 5 days of issue
⏰ CRITICAL DEADLINE

Service must be effected within 5 days of the case being issued. If serving the State, also serve the State Attorney.

Valid under Rule 9(1)(a)(iv). The respondent must have an email address.

  • Attach all documents as PDFs
  • Subject: Service of Labour Court Application - Case No: [number]
  • Request a read receipt
  • Keep the sent email in your inbox

Send to the respondent's registered address or place of business.

  • Use registered mail with tracking number
  • Keep the postal receipt
  • Service deemed 7 days after posting (Rule 9(3))

Hand documents directly to the respondent or a person apparently in charge at their place of business.

Proof of Service
File proof that service was effected
  • Screenshot of sent email showing recipient address
  • Read receipt or delivery confirmation
  • PDF copies of all attachments sent
  • Email has not bounced back
  • Postal receipt with tracking number
  • Copy of documents sent
  • Proof of delivery (if available)
Return of Service Affidavit
File an affidavit confirming how, when, and on whom service was effected
