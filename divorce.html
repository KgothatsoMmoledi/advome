📚 Relevant Legislation
Divorce Act 70 of 1979 Section 4-8
Governs the grounds for divorce, division of assets, and maintenance. Irretrievable breakdown of marriage is the primary ground.
Primary Legislation
Children's Act 38 of 2005 Section 18-33
Governs parental responsibilities and rights, parenting plans, and the best interests of the child standard. The Family Advocate must approve parenting plans in contested matters.

Divorce Questions

Is the divorce defended or undefended?
Check if a plea was filed within 10 days of service. No plea = undefended. Plea filed = defended.
What about the children?
If there are minor children, a parenting plan is required. The Family Advocate must approve it in contested matters.
Can we settle?
Settlement is always possible, even in defended matters. A signed settlement agreement can be made an order of court.
