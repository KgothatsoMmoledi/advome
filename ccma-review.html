Intake & referral

What the user must tell the engine

  • Dispute typeDismissal, unfair labour practice, discrimination, wages, probation, or mixed matter.
  • Key datesDismissal date, act/omission date, date of knowledge, and whether the referral is late.
  • Forum checkConfirm whether CCMA, a bargaining council, or another forum is the right route.
  • Service proofRequire proof that the referral was served on the other party.
Conciliation & settlement

How the page should behave

  • Settlement stays visibleSettlement should remain available at every stage, not just at the end.
  • Neutral wordingAsk what happened and whether the user felt heard, not whether anyone acted unlawfully.
  • Readiness meterShow a simple readiness score with missing documents and urgent actions.
  • Outcome gateIf the conciliation fails, unlock the award and review pathways.
Arbitration workspace

Hearing prep cards

  • Statement of caseWrite the version of events and requested remedy.
  • Evidence bundleUpload documents, letters, payslips, screenshots, and messages.
  • Witness listTrack names, roles, and whether they can attend.
  • Transcript / audioAllow audio notes, voice-to-text drafting, and later transcription support.
Review challenge centre

After the award

When the award is received, the first question should be simple and neutral: Are you happy with the award?

  • Choose the issueProcedural unfairness, ignored evidence, wrong jurisdiction, misconduct, or unreasonable result.
  • Deadline trackerShow the review clock immediately and highlight urgency.
  • Record and transcriptTrack audio, transcription, pagination, and indexing for the review file.
  • Motion sequenceNotice of motion, founding affidavit, response, reply, record, heads, and set-down.
Review grounds

What the engine can help organise

Use this as a checklist rather than legal advice.

  • MisconductWhat went wrong in the process?
  • Gross irregularityWas the hearing fundamentally unfair?
  • Excess of powersDid the decision-maker go beyond the role?

UI behaviour rules

Build principles for this page

  • Always show the stageThe user should never wonder where the matter is.
  • Always show the next stepMake the path obvious and calm.
  • Always show urgencyDeadlines and missing papers should be visible.
  • Always keep settlement availableSettlement should remain open in every relevant state.
  • Stay neutralThe platform can help organise a challenge without accusing anyone.
