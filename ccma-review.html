Section 34 — Access to Justice
The right to a fair hearing includes the right to challenge decisions that are unlawful or procedurally unfair. The review mechanism exists to protect that right.
⚖️ Grounds of Review (Section 145 LRA)

These are the legal grounds on which a CCMA award may be challenged in the Labour Court. Advome provides these categories for information only. We do not assess whether your specific award falls into any category.

Misconduct

Something improper happened in the arbitration process — bias, conflict of interest, or procedural violation.

Information only
Gross Irregularity

The hearing procedure was fundamentally unfair — evidence excluded, no right to reply, or commissioner overstepped.

Information only
Excess of Powers

The commissioner made a decision they had no legal authority to make, or ignored binding precedent.

Information only
📚 Relevant Legislation
Labour Relations Act 66 of 1995 Section 145
Provides for the review of arbitration awards by the Labour Court on grounds of misconduct, gross irregularity, or excess of powers. The application must be lodged within 6 weeks of the award being served.
Primary Legislation Labour Court
Labour Court Rules Rule 37
Governs the preparation, pagination, indexing, and filing of the review record. Rule 37(12) deals with necessary portions vs complete record. Rule 37(13) governs Court Online upload.

Review Questions

What happened in the award?
Summarise the result in plain language: compensation, reinstatement, dismissal upheld, or partial relief.
Do you want to challenge it?
If the answer is no, return to the Award page and accept. If yes, identify your grounds and proceed to motion. Consult an attorney if unsure.
What documents are missing?
Flag the record, reasons, and dates. The motion page will track these as you progress.
