Dashboard Questions

How do I know if my CCMA award should be challenged?
Check the award page. If the decision is not acceptable, open the review flow. The dashboard will then show the matter as a review track.
What does the readiness score mean?
It measures document completeness, deadline proximity, and stage progression. Critical = immediate action needed. Attention = monitor closely. Healthy = on track.
Can I share my dashboard with an attorney?
Yes. Use the Attorney Connection feature. You choose which matters and documents to share. The attorney sees only what you permit.
