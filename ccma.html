Common Questions

What do I bring to conciliation?
Bring referral papers, identification, and any documents supporting your dispute.
What if the matter does not settle?
The workflow moves into arbitration, then award, then review if the award is challenged.
How do I know if I am ready?
The health meter checks documents, notices, hearing readiness, and bundle completeness.
