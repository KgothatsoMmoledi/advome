The Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration (CCMA) is a South African dispute resolution body established under the Labour Relations Act 66 of 1995 (LRA). It helps employees and employers resolve workplace disputes without going to court.

Key facts:

  • Conciliation is free for all parties (LRA Section 135)
  • You must file a referral within 30 days of dismissal (LRA Section 191)
  • No lawyers are required at conciliation — you can represent yourself
  • CCMA has offices in all major cities
  • Most disputes resolve at conciliation stage
  • Constructive dismissal, unfair labour practice, and wage disputes are all valid referrals
  1. Referral — File LRA Form 7.11 within 30 days (Section 191)
  2. Conciliation — Attempt settlement with commissioner (Section 135)
  3. Arbitration — Formal hearing if no settlement (Section 138)
  4. Award — Arbitrator issues binding decision (Section 138)
  5. Review — Challenge award in Labour Court (Section 145, 6 weeks)
  6. Motion — Apply for leave to appeal
  7. Record — Request arbitration record
  8. Finalisation — Enforce or conclude
  • Unfair dismissal (LRA Section 188) — Dismissal without fair reason or procedure
  • Automatically unfair dismissal (LRA Section 187) — Dismissal for pregnancy, union activity, whistleblowing, etc.
  • Constructive dismissal (LRA Section 186(e)) — Resignation due to intolerable conditions
  • Unfair labour practice (LRA Section 186(2)) — Unfair suspension, demotion, benefit denial
  • Wage / salary disputes — Unilateral changes to remuneration
  • Discrimination (Employment Equity Act) — Unfair treatment based on prohibited grounds
  • Sexual harassment — Protected disclosure or direct referral
  • No written contract? — Verbal agreements are valid employment contracts under South African common law and the LRA. Payslips, attendance records, and witness testimony can prove employment.

Important Deadlines

30-Day Rule (LRA s191)
File your referral within 30 days of dismissal. Late referrals need condonation — difficult to obtain.
6-Week Challenge (LRA s145)
Challenge an award within 6 weeks of service date. No extensions.
