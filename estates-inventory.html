5. Questions panel

The user should be able to ask inventory questions directly from this page.

What counts as an estate asset?

Anything owned by the deceased that has value, such as property, money, vehicles, or investments.

Do I need valuations for everything?

The page should ask for an estimated or confirmed value for each major asset where possible.

What debts should be listed?

List debts, finance agreements, tax obligations, utilities, and other liabilities of the estate.

What happens after inventory is complete?

The workflow should move to the estate bank account and then into advertising and creditor claims.
