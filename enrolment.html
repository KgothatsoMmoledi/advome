📝 Practice Manual (Section 18)

Once the Registrar allocates a hearing date, the matter is considered enrolled. The Registrar notifies all parties. If no date is allocated within a reasonable time, you must follow up.

When to follow up

If more than 10 court days have passed since you applied for a hearing date and no allocation has been made, send a polite follow‑up email. If still no response after a further 5 court days, escalate to the Court Manager.

Enrolment Checklist
Confirm each step to ensure your matter is properly enrolled
  • Request for hearing date filed with Registrar
  • Proof of filing uploaded / retained
  • Check Court Online for allocation updates
  • Follow‑up email sent (if no allocation after 10 court days)
  • Escalation to Court Manager (if no response after further 5 court days)
  • Hearing date allocated and all parties notified
