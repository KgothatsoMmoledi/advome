Step 19 of 21 — Apply for a hearing date, track allocation, and follow up with the Registrar

Labour Court
1. Apply for a Hearing Date
Rule 37(25) — You must apply within 10 court days after pagination
⏰ 10‑DAY DEADLINE

File your request for a hearing date within 10 court days of completing the index and pagination. The Registrar will then allocate a date.

📖 The Law

Rule 37(25): After indexing and paginating the file, the applicant must index and paginate the file and, within 10 court days, apply to the Registrar for a hearing date.

Practice Manual, Section 18: The Registrar is responsible for allocating hearing dates. Once allocated, the matter is considered enrolled. The Registrar notifies all parties.

  • Draft the request – use the template below or generate one with the AI.
  • Sign the document.
  • Submit it to the Registrar via Court Online (upload under your case) or by email (find the Registrar's email address on the court's website).
  • Keep proof of submission (screenshot, email copy, or confirmation).
IN THE LABOUR COURT OF SOUTH AFRICA (HELD AT [SEAT]) Case No: [CASE NUMBER] In the matter between: [APPLICANT NAME] — Applicant and First Respondent: [CCMA] Second Respondent: [EMPLOYER NAME] Third Respondent: [COMMISSIONER NAME] REQUEST FOR HEARING DATE TO: The Registrar of the Labour Court The Applicant hereby applies for a hearing date in the above review application. The complete file has been indexed and paginated in accordance with Rule 29(3) and Rule 37(25), consisting of: • Section A: Pleadings and Affidavits — [X] pages • Section B: Notices and Other Documents — [X] pages • Section C: The Record — [X] pages (Volume [X] of [Y]) The matter is [opposed / unopposed]. All parties have been duly served. Proof of service is attached. The Applicant requests that the matter be set down for hearing on the earliest available date. SIGNED at [PLACE] on this [DAY] day of [MONTH] [YEAR]. ___________________________ Signature [NAME IN BLOCK LETTERS]

📧 When to follow up

If 10 court days pass without a hearing date, send a polite follow‑up email. If still no response after a further 5 court days, escalate to the Senior Registrar / Court Manager.

Enrolment Checklist
Ensure every step is completed
  • File indexed and paginated (Sections A, B, C)
  • Request for hearing date drafted and signed
  • Request filed with Registrar (Court Online or email)
  • Proof of filing kept
  • Check Court Online for allocation updates
  • Follow‑up email sent (if no date after 10 court days)
  • Escalation to Court Manager (if still no response)
  • Hearing date allocated and all parties notified
