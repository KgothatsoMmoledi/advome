Questions

The page should show the bundle, authorities, witness notes, and any directives or practice notes.

What happens if the matter is not called?

The page should show the waiting status and whether a settlement or adjournment is likely.

Can the matter still settle on trial day?

Yes. Settlement should always remain visible as a live possibility.

What if the judge gives directives?

The directives section should become the place to track any court instructions after appearance.
