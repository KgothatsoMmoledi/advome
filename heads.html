Heads of Argument — Manual Preparation
Rule 40 & Practice Manual — File within 15 court days
⏰ 15‑DAY DEADLINE (opposed matters)

If the review is opposed, your Heads of Argument must be filed within 15 court days after the pleadings close (Rule 40). For unopposed matters, they are usually filed before the hearing.

📖 The Law

Rule 40(1): In every opposed matter, the applicant must deliver heads of argument not later than 15 court days after the close of pleadings.

Rule 40(2): The respondent must deliver heads of argument not later than 10 court days before the hearing.

Practice Manual: Heads must be clear, concise, and set out the main points intended to be argued. They must list the authorities relied upon.

  • Draft the document using the AI generator or manually with the official structure above.
  • Sign the heads (counsel or self‑represented applicant).
  • Serve a copy on all other parties.
  • File the original with the Registrar (via Court Online or email).
  • Keep proof of filing and service.
📝 What Happens Next?
  1. After filing, the respondent will file their heads (if opposed).
  2. The matter will be enrolled for hearing once allocation is complete.
  3. You will prepare for oral argument on the day of the hearing.
