Heads Questions

What are the key legal issues?
Identify the review questions the court must decide: misconduct, gross irregularity, or excess of powers.
Which cases or laws matter?
Attach authorities and legislation that support each ground of review. Cross-reference to the record.
Is the matter opposed?
Track whether respondent heads are expected. The workflow adapts based on answering affidavit, not just notice of intention.
