5. Questions panel

The user should be able to ask advertising and claims questions directly from this page.

What should the creditor notice say?

It should identify the estate, the executor, the claim period, and where claims must be lodged.

What if a creditor objects?

The page should mark the claim or objection as disputed and keep it visible before the L&D account is finalised.

When do I draft the L&D account?

After advertising, the waiting period, and claim handling are sufficiently complete.

What happens after the claim period?

The workflow should move into the Liquidation and Distribution account and then toward final distribution.
