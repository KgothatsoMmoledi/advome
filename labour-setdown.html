Set-Down Questions

Is the matter opposed?
Track whether opposition papers were filed. Answering affidavit = fully opposed. Notice only = partial.
Has the file been indexed?
Confirm bundle order and pagination. The Registrar will check this before allocation.
Is the registrar ready to allocate?
Track the enrolment request state. Follow up if delayed. Escalate to court manager if unreasonable.
Disclaimer This module provides procedural assistance and legal information for labour disputes. It does not provide legal advice. Users remain responsible for complying with Labour Court Rules and the Labour Relations Act. Advome does not create, assign, or guarantee hearing dates. The Registrar controls allocation.
Section 34 of the Constitution protects the right of access to justice. Advome supports that right through procedural guidance and structured information — not through the unauthorised practice of law.
