Section 34 — Access to Justice
Every person has the right to have any dispute that can be resolved by law decided in a fair public hearing. The review process exists to protect that right when the award is flawed.

Questions About the Award

What did the award say?
Capture the result in a short summary: was the dismissal upheld, compensation ordered, or reinstatement granted?
When was it served?
The review clock starts from service, not the date on the award. Record this accurately.
Do you want to challenge it?
If the award is wrong on law, fact, or procedure, the review path is available within 6 weeks of service.
