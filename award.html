Understanding Your Award

No Written Contract?
Verbal employment agreements are valid and enforceable. The CCMA and Labour Court accept payslips, bank records, and witness testimony as proof of employment.
Reinstatement (LRA s193)
If reinstated, you return to your job with back pay from dismissal date.
Compensation (LRA s194)
Capped at 12 months for ordinary dismissal; 24 months for automatically unfair dismissal (LRA s187).
No Order as to Costs
Each party usually pays their own costs. The CCMA does not award costs against the losing party.
