Review Application Required

Challenging an award requires an application to the Labour Court. This is a complex process.

Grounds for review (limited):

  • Arbitrator exceeded their powers
  • Arbitrator committed misconduct
  • Arbitrator made a grossly irregular decision
  • Arbitrator improperly admitted or excluded evidence

Warning: Labour Court is now compulsory online via Court Online. You must file electronically.

Review Requirements Checklist

  • File within 6 weeks of service date
    Late applications require condonation — difficult to obtain
  • Identify specific review grounds
    Vague complaints will be dismissed
  • Request arbitration record from CCMA
    You need the full transcript to identify errors
  • Register on Court Online
    Labour Court filings are compulsory online
  • Draft founding affidavit
    Set out facts, legal grounds, and relief sought
  • Pay Labour Court filing fee
    R0 for disputes under R50,000; higher for larger claims

Understanding Your Award

Reinstatement
If reinstated, you return to your job with back pay from dismissal date.
Compensation
Capped at 12 months' remuneration for ordinary dismissal; 24 months for automatically unfair dismissal.
No Order as to Costs
Each party usually pays their own costs. The CCMA does not award costs against the losing party.
