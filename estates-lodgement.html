1. Covering letter builder

Tell the user what to write, what to attach, and how to keep a stamped copy for proof of lodgement.

Drafting

The letter should say

  • What is being applied for
  • Who the deceased is
  • Who is lodging the file
  • What documents are attached
  • The estate reference and contact details

Proof of application

  • Print two copies
  • Lodge one copy
  • Keep one stamped copy
  • Store the stamp as proof of lodgement

3. Route selection

The engine should help the user choose the correct appointment route.

If there is a will

  • Apply for Letter of Executorship
  • Confirm the named executor
  • Attach the will and supporting forms

If no will or a small estate route applies

  • Apply for Letter of Authority
  • Confirm the smaller administrative route
  • Use the correct forms for that route

6. Questions panel

The user should be able to ask lodgement questions directly from this page.

What goes in the covering letter?

It should state what is being applied for, what is attached, and who the deceased and applicant are.

Why do I need a stamped copy?

The stamped copy is proof that the file was lodged with the Master’s Office.

What if the Master asks for more documents?

The file should stay marked incomplete until the missing items are added and resubmitted.

Do I need executorship or authority?

The correct route depends on the will, the estate structure, and the appointment path chosen by the Master.
