For constructive dismissal, this is the date you resigned.
This will appear on your referral form. Keep it under 500 words. Focus on facts, not emotions.
  1. CCMA sends you a case number within 48 hours
  2. CCMA notifies your employer (respondent) via registered mail or email
  3. A conciliation date is set (usually within 30 days, LRA s135)
  4. You receive a Notice of Set Down with date, time, and venue
  5. Both parties must attend conciliation (LRA s135)
  6. If no settlement: matter proceeds to arbitration (LRA s138)
