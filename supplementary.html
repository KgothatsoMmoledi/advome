Supplementary Affidavit (Optional)
File within 5 days of the record being filed
📋 When to File

File within 5 days of the record being filed (Rule 37(20)). This is optional — only file if the record reveals new facts or grounds not in your founding affidavit.

Common Reasons for Supplementary Affidavit

• The transcribed record reveals an error not apparent from the award
• New documentary evidence discovered
• Need to correct a factual error in founding affidavit
• Address a specific legal point raised in the record

✅ Skip This Step

If your founding affidavit is complete and the record does not reveal new issues, you can skip the supplementary affidavit and proceed directly to the Answering Affidavit step.
