Supplementary Affidavit

Step 13 of 21 — Optional: supplement your review grounds after reading the record

Labour Court

✅ This Step is Completely Optional

You are not required to file a Supplementary Affidavit. Under Rule 37(20), you may simply file a Notice of Standing By your Notice of Motion and proceed to the next step. Skip this page if your founding affidavit already covers all your grounds.

Do I Need a Supplementary Affidavit?
Rule 37(20) — Decide within 5 court days of the record being filed

📖 The Law — Rule 37(20)

Within 5 court days after the transcribed record has been filed, the Applicant must do ONE of the following:

  • (a) Deliver a Notice of Amendment and Supplementary Affidavit to supplement the grounds for review; OR
  • (b) Deliver a Notice that the Applicant stands by its Notice of Motion.

Rule 37(21): A supplementary affidavit may do no more than supplement the grounds for review recorded in the founding affidavit or abandon any one or more of them. It cannot introduce entirely new, unrelated issues.

⚠️ Warning: Costs Risk

An applicant who abuses the supplementary affidavit by including irrelevant or repetitive material risks an adverse order as to costs (Rule 37(21)). Only use this if the transcribed record reveals something genuinely new.

  • I have read the complete transcribed record
  • The record reveals a factual error or omission not in my founding affidavit
  • I need to correct or clarify a specific paragraph in my founding affidavit
  • I want to abandon one or more of my review grounds
  • The record contains evidence that strengthens an existing ground for review

⏭️ Skip This Step

If your founding affidavit covers everything, you do not need a supplementary affidavit.

Notice of Standing By
Rule 37(20)(b) — Use this if you do NOT need a supplementary affidavit
✅ This is the Normal Path

Most applicants do not need a supplementary affidavit. If your founding affidavit is complete, simply file this Notice of Standing By within 5 court days of the record being filed. This tells the Court and the Respondents that you rely on your founding affidavit as it stands.

📖 Effect of Filing This Notice

Once you file a Notice of Standing By:

  • The Respondent has 10 court days from receipt to file an Answering Affidavit (Rule 37(22)).
  • You do not need to file anything further until you receive the Respondent's Answering Affidavit.
  • If no Answering Affidavit is filed, the matter may proceed as unopposed.

File & Serve
Rule 37(20) & Rule 9 — File within 5 court days of the record
⏰ 5-DAY DEADLINE

You must file either your Supplementary Affidavit + Notice of Amendment OR your Notice of Standing By within 5 court days of the transcribed record being filed. This is not optional.

  • First Respondent (CCMA): Serve at the CCMA's registered address or email. The Commissioner is served at the same address unless otherwise indicated.
  • Second Respondent (Employer): Serve at the employer's registered office, place of business, or email address (Rule 9(1)(b)(i)).
  • Third Respondent (Commissioner): Serve at the same address as the CCMA unless a different address has been indicated.
  • Email Service (Rule 9(1)(a)(iv)): If the respondent has an email address, serve by email. Request a read receipt. Keep the sent email.
  • Proof of Service (Rule 9(2)(b)): Draft an affidavit confirming the correct email address, that the whole document was sent, and that a named person acknowledged receipt.
  • Hand Delivery (Rule 9(1)(a)(i)): Hand to the party or an authorised representative. Obtain a signed acknowledgement of receipt.
